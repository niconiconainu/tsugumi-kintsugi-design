What is Kintsugi

The art of golden repair.

photo: kintsugi bowl detail, gold seams

Kintsugi (金継ぎ), "golden joinery," is a centuries-old Japanese craft of repairing broken pottery with lacquer dusted in gold. Rather than hiding the damage, the break becomes part of the object's story.

It reflects the spirit of wabi-sabi — finding beauty in imperfection, and worth in what has endured. Tsugumi honors this tradition by connecting your piece with artisans trained in its practice.
